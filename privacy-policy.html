Damodar Dhakal Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Damodar Dhakal handles information when you visit this website and use its tools.

Information We Collect

The website is designed to work without requiring an account. Information you enter into a tool is processed only to provide that tool's result. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through these tools.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate, maintain, improve, and protect the website and its tools. Data entered into a calculator or lookup tool is not used to identify you.

Cookies and Third-Party Services

This website does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or tracking. External services, if added in the future, may have their own privacy policies.

Data Retention and Security

Information entered into browser-based tools generally remains in your browser and is not stored by this website. Reasonable measures are used to protect the website, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

Your Rights

You may choose not to provide information and may stop using the website at any time. You can clear locally stored website data through your browser settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the latest revision was made.

Contact

For privacy questions about this website, use the contact method provided on the Damodar Dhakal homepage.
